Finding Nature in Everyday Life

October 2, 2026 by Janae Forbes

Nature is all around us, but sometimes we become so busy with school, work, technology, and our daily responsibilities that we forget to notice it. A beautiful sunset, the sound of birds in the morning, a cool breeze, or a tree blooming with flowers can easily become something we walk past without a second thought.

This blog is about slowing down and appreciating those little moments. I created this blog to explore the connection between nature and our everyday lives. I will be sharing thoughts, experiences, and ideas about spending time outdoors, enjoying green spaces, bringing nature into our homes, and finding simple ways to live a little more sustainably.

Nature does not always require a trip into the mountains or a long hike. Sometimes, it can be found right outside our homes or along the road we take every day. Through this blog, I hope to encourage others to pay a little more attention to the natural world around them. There is beauty in ordinary places — we just have to take the time to notice it.

5 Ways to Bring Nature into Your Home

September 15, 2026 by Janae Forbes

Bringing nature into your home can create a calming and refreshing environment. Here are five simple ways to do it:

  1. Houseplants: Add some greenery with indoor plants.
  2. Natural Light: Open your curtains and let the sunlight in.
  3. Nature-Inspired Decor: Use natural materials like wood and stone.
  4. Fresh Flowers: Keep a vase of fresh flowers on your table.
  5. Outdoor Views: Arrange your furniture to enjoy views of the outdoors.

By incorporating these elements, you can create a space that feels connected to nature, even when you're indoors.
